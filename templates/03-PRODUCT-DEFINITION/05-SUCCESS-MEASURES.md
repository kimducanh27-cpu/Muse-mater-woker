## Rules
- Never invent a baseline, target, sample size or benchmark.
- If a baseline is unknown, use UNKNOWN or BASELINE NEEDED.
- Target status must be UNKNOWN, HYPOTHESIS or COMMITTED.
- COMMITTED requires a defensible baseline, measurement source and rationale.
- Keep owner and guardrails visible in the canonical Product Definition.
- Include leading and lagging measures where meaningful; do not force vanity metrics.
