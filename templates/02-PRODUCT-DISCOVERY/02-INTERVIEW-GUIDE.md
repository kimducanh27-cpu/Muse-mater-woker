## Opening and consent
- Explain purpose: learn about experience, not sell a concept.
- Confirm time and permission to take notes/record.
- Explain how notes will be used and anonymized.
- Do not collect unnecessary sensitive or identifying information.

## Warm-up
1. Tell me about your role / context related to this activity.
2. How often does this situation come up?

## Recent concrete experience
1. Think about the last time you [did the job]. Can you walk me through what happened?
2. What triggered it?
3. What did you do first, and what happened next?
4. Which tools, people or workarounds did you use?
5. Where did you lose time, make a trade-off or get stuck?
6. What did you try previously? Why did you stop or continue?

## Importance and alternatives
1. What happens when this problem is not solved?
2. How do you handle it today?
3. Have you spent time or money trying to improve this?
4. What is already working well in the current solution?

## Close
1. What important part of this process have I not asked about?
2. Is there another person who regularly experiences this situation?
3. May I contact you for a follow-up, if needed?

## Interviewer reminders
- Ask about the participant's life/work, not whether they like the idea.
- Ask about the past, not speculative future behavior.
- Avoid leading questions, pitching, hypotheticals and feature requests as solutions.
- Ask for specific examples and what happened next.
- Distinguish what the participant did from what they say they might do.
- Compliments and politeness are not validation.
