# TAB 02 — DEEP RESEARCH

Use `seed-research`. This is a new conversation, not a continuation of Tab 01. Workspace artifacts are the source of truth.

Read first:
- `product/seed/01_SEED_CANDIDATE.md`
- `product/seed/03_DECISION_LOG.md`

Do not begin unless Gate 1 approval is explicitly recorded. If missing, stop and identify the exact blocker.

Research only what could change the product decision:
1. Problem/user evidence and real-world workarounds.
2. Direct competitors, indirect substitutes and non-app solutions.
3. Relevant GitHub/open-source projects, actual scope, license and maintenance.
4. Android platform constraints tied to the proposed mechanism.
5. Highest-impact assumptions, evidence for and against, and cheapest credible tests.

Evidence requirements:
- Prefer primary sources and inspect the source itself.
- Search snippets are not final evidence.
- Record URL, owner/publisher, visible publish/update date and date checked.
- Separate FACT / USER-REPORTED / INTERPRETATION / HYPOTHESIS.
- State each source's limitation. Do not invent market size, adoption, ratings or performance numbers.
- Mark unconfirmed facts `NOT VERIFIED` and unsuccessful searches `NOT FOUND`.

Create `product/seed/02_RESEARCH_DOSSIER.md` from the template. Do not rewrite the candidate or silently change the decision log.

End with a Gate 2 brief and wait for exactly one of:
- `APPROVE GATE 2`
- `MORE RESEARCH: [specific question]`
- `RETURN TO EXPLORE: [reason]`

Do not start Tab 03.
