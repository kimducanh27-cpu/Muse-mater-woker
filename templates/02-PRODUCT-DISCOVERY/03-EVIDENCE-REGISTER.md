## Evidence quality checks
- Is this first-hand or second-hand?
- Is it behavior, opinion, recall or interpretation?
- Is the source relevant to the target segment?
- What is the sample size and recruitment method?
- What contradicts this evidence?
- What cannot be concluded from this source?
