# Assumption Map

## Risk categories
- Value — Will this solve an important user problem?
- Usability — Can users understand and use the proposed mechanism?
- Feasibility — Can the team/platform build and operate it?
- Viability — Can the product be sustained and fit the relevant business/legal constraints?
- Ethics — Could it harm users, misuse data or create unfair outcomes?
- Go-to-market — Can the intended users be reached and activated?
- Strategy — Does the opportunity fit the product's intended direction?
- Team — Are required skills, access and resources available?

For an existing product, prioritize the four core risks first; include additional categories only when relevant.

## Priority logic
- HIGH impact + HIGH uncertainty: test before committing.
- HIGH impact + LOW uncertainty: preserve evidence and monitor.
- LOW impact + HIGH uncertainty: test only if cheap or dependent on a high-priority risk.
- LOW impact + LOW uncertainty: defer.

Use qualitative levels unless the underlying data supports numeric estimates. Do not imply a mathematically precise rank from subjective scores.
